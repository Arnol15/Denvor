Vertical transportation

A dedicated lifts & escalators practice

Vertical transportation is a safety-critical discipline in its own right, not a side task for a general maintenance crew — so Denrova runs it as a standalone practice with its own technicians, schedules and inspection records.

Kenya's lift and escalator population is growing with every new commercial tower, hospital, mall and residential block — and every one of those units needs a maintenance provider who treats compliance and passenger safety as non-negotiable.

Denrova services passenger, goods and hydraulic lifts alongside escalators and moving walkways, across new installations and legacy equipment due for modernization. Every unit on contract gets a maintenance history, a defined service interval, and a technician who knows it.

Because a stalled lift or an unresolved fault is a liability, not just an inconvenience, our response times and documentation are built around minimizing the time any unit spends out of service.

Maintenance
Scheduled preventive servicing for passenger, goods and hydraulic lifts, escalators and moving walkways.
Callout response
Rapid attendance for breakdowns and entrapments, with fault records kept against each unit.
Safety inspection
Routine technical inspection and compliance checks aligned with recognised lift safety standards.
Modernization
Controller, drive and door-operator upgrades that extend the life of ageing installations.
New installation
Supply and installation of new lift and escalator equipment, commissioned to handover.
Service contracts
Fixed-interval maintenance agreements for building owners, facility managers and developers.
Automation & control

Automation built for uptime, not novelty

Automation only earns its cost when it makes a plant more predictable — fewer surprises, clearer fault data, and processes that run the same way on every shift.

Denrova's automation work spans new-line PLC programming through to retrofitting relay-logic machinery with modern, supportable controls — the kind of upgrade that quietly removes years of nuisance downtime.

We design and build the control panel to go with it, wire it to the standard your insurer and safety officer expect, and hand over documentation an in-house electrician can actually work from.

PLC programming
Ladder logic and structured text programs for new lines and machine upgrades.
Control panels
Panel design, build and wiring to switchgear and safety standards.
SCADA & monitoring
Visibility into production and utility systems from a single interface.
Retrofits
Replacing obsolete relay logic and drives with current, supportable controls.

Quality engineering

How we hold the line on quality

An engineering firm is only as good as its worst job. These are the habits that keep every job at the same standard, regardless of who's on site.

Documented process

Every job runs against a checklist and closes with a written report; findings, work done and parts used, not a verbal summary.

Tested before handover

Installations and repairs are load-tested and function-checked before we call a job complete, not after the client finds the fault.

Standards-aligned

Work on lifts, electrical systems and pressure equipment follows recognised safety and engineering standards, not shortcuts.

Trained technicians

Field staff are trained on the specific equipment they service, with technical training extended to client teams as well.
